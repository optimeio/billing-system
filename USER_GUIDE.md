# 📘 SM Groups Billing & Inventory System - User Guide

Welcome to the **SM Groups** management platform. This system is a premium, all-in-one solution for billing, inventory tracking, HR management, and internal communication.

---

## 🔐 Getting Started: Login & Security

### 1. Accessing the System
*   Open your browser and navigate to the system URL.
*   Enter your **Email or Staff ID** and your **Password**.

### 2. First-Time Login (Security OTP)
For your security, the first time you log in (or after an Admin resets your password):
*   The system will send a **6-digit Security Code (OTP)** to your registered email.
*   Enter this code and set your **Permanent Password**.
*   *Note: This ensures only you have access to your account.*

---

## 👑 1. Admin Guide (Superuser)

As an Admin, you have full oversight of the entire organization.

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
*   View real-time **Revenue**, **Staff Activity**, and **Low Stock Alerts**.
*   The system updates automatically when an invoice is created anywhere in the system.

### 👥 Staff Management
*   **Add Staff**: Create new accounts for team members. Assign roles: `Admin`, `Inventory`, or `Staff`.
*   **Block/Unblock**: Instantly disable access for any staff member.
*   **Reset Passwords**: If a staff member forgets their password, you can trigger a reset.

### 📦 Inventory Oversight
*   **Categories**: Organize your products into logical groups (e.g., Electronics, Grocery).
*   **Products**: Add, edit, or delete products. Monitor stock levels globally.

### 💰 Finance & Verification
*   **Invoices**: View all invoices generated by staff. Download PDF copies for your records.
*   **Payment Queue**: Approve or reject manual/offline payments submitted by staff.
*   **Expense Approval**: Review staff expense claims. Approved expenses are tracked in your financial reports.

### 📢 Communication & HR
*   **Leave Management**: View leave history for all staff. Approve or Reject requests (this sends an automatic email to the staff).
*   **Announcements**: 
    *   **Global**: Send a message to everyone.
    *   **Targeted**: Select specific staff members for private notices.
    *   *Every announcement sends an immediate email to recipients.*

---

## 📦 2. Inventory Admin Guide (Manager)

The Inventory Manager focuses on stock integrity and product flow.

### 📑 Product Control
*   **Manage Inventory**: Update stock counts when new shipments arrive.
*   **Barcode/SKU**: Ensure every product has a unique ID for accurate tracking.
*   **Categories**: Keep the category list clean and organized.

### 📦 Low Stock Alerts
*   Monitor the dashboard for **Stock Alerts**. If an item falls below 10 units, it will turn red.

---

## 📝 3. Staff Guide (Billing & Operations)

Staff members are the primary operators for sales and daily tasks.

### 🧾 Billing & Invoicing
*   **Create Invoice**: Use the "Create Invoice" screen to add customer details and line items.
*   **Real-time Stock**: You cannot bill an item that is out of stock. The system protects your inventory integrity.
*   **My Invoices**: View and download PDFs of the invoices you have generated.

### 💸 Expenses & HR
*   **Apply for Expense**: Submit claims for business costs (e.g., travel, supplies). Attach details and wait for Admin approval.
*   **Leave Request**: Use the "Leave Request" form to apply for time off. You can track your request status (Pending/Approved/Rejected) in your history.

### 📱 Scanner & Verification
*   **Generate QR**: Generate a unique session QR code for attendance or entry verification. The Admin will scan this to verify your session.

### 💬 Notice Board (Announcements)
*   Check the **Announcements** tab for updates from the Admin.
*   **Threaded Replies**: Click on a notice to reply to the Admin. You can choose to "Reply to All" or keep your conversation private with the Admin.

---

## ❓ Troubleshooting & Support

*   **Page Not Found (404)**: Ensure you are logged in. If the error persists, contact the Admin to verify your role permissions.
*   **Email Not Received**: Check your "Spam" folder. Ensure your email is spelled correctly in your profile.
*   **Stock Issues**: If you cannot find a product while billing, verify with the Inventory Manager that the product is active and in stock.
